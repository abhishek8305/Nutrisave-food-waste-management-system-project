NutriSave Help Center

Your guide to reducing food waste and supporting the community.

1. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I donate food?
Register as a donor and upload details including the food item name, quantity, and expiry time via the "Add Food" section.
How do I collect food?
NGOs and volunteers can view available food listings and submit a pickup request through the dashboard.
How do I track a donation?
Use the System Tracking feature in your "Donations Status" menu to monitor current progress and delivery updates.
What are the requirements?
Ensure you have a stable internet connection and use a modern browser like Chrome or Edge for the best experience.

2. How NutriSave Works

  • Listing: Restaurants list surplus food with real-time images and expiry data.
  • Matching: Our system notifies nearby registered NGOs about the availability.
  • Verification: NGOs verify the food quality upon pickup using the digital checklist.
  • Completion: Once delivered, the status is updated to 'Completed' for record-keeping.

3. Contact Support

Technical Issues: If you experience bugs with the Python backend or MySQL database, please email dev@nutrisave.com with a detailed report.

Location Assistance: trouble finding a pickup location? Call our hotline: 1-800-NUTRI.

Account Help: For registration or login issues, contact support@nutrisave.com.

4. Safety & Guidelines

  • Food Expiry: Always check the listed food expiry time before requesting a pickup to ensure the food reaches the needy while edible.
  • Quality Standards: Donors are responsible for providing accurate details and maintaining hygiene during packing.
  • Coordination: Maintain clear communication via the platform to reduce transit time and prevent wastage.
